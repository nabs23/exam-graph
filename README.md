# ExamGraph

**ExamGraph** is an intelligent exam preparation and mastery platform that transforms official examination syllabi and Tables of Specifications into structured, concept-driven learning paths.

Instead of treating review materials as a simple collection of topics and questions, ExamGraph models the relationships between **syllabus areas, concepts, prerequisites, lessons, learning objectives, questions, standards, assessments, and student mastery**.

At the center of the system is a concept graph. Official syllabus topics define the examination scope, while individual concepts represent the knowledge and skills a learner must actually understand. These concepts can be connected through prerequisite relationships, mapped to lessons and learning objectives, and assessed through targeted questions.

ExamGraph uses this structure to determine not only whether a student answered a question correctly, but also **which concepts and learning objectives the question measures**. Student performance can therefore be tracked at the concept level, allowing the system to identify weaknesses, recommend remediation, select appropriate practice questions, and build increasingly adaptive study experiences.

The platform is designed around three complementary structures:

- **Syllabus Tree** — represents the official scope and hierarchy defined by the examination authority.
- **Concept Graph** — represents the underlying knowledge, dependencies, and relationships that learners must master.
- **Exam Blueprint** — represents the examination's required coverage, weighting, cognitive levels, and difficulty distribution.

These structures remain independent but interconnected, allowing ExamGraph to preserve official examination requirements while supporting more flexible instructional and adaptive-learning models.

ExamGraph is initially being developed around the **Philippine CPA Licensure Examination (CPALE)**, with Financial Accounting and Reporting serving as the first complete implementation model. However, the platform architecture is exam-independent and can support additional professional licensure examinations, certifications, academic assessments, and other syllabus-driven learning programs.

Core capabilities include structured syllabus management, concept extraction and mapping, lesson and learning-objective management, question-bank development, exam-blueprint alignment, standards and regulatory versioning, diagnostic assessments, concept-level mastery tracking, remediation, and adaptive practice.

Future capabilities can extend the platform with AI-assisted curriculum extraction, AI-generated and validated questions, misconception detection, spaced repetition, semantic retrieval, psychometric item analysis, knowledge tracing, and graph-based adaptive recommendations.

**ExamGraph turns an exam syllabus into a navigable knowledge graph—and student performance into a measurable path toward mastery.**
