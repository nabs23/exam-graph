# 06 — CPALE Official Syllabus & TOS Baseline (2022–2028)

## Purpose
This resource is the canonical curriculum baseline for the Reviewer App's CPALE implementation.

It normalizes **PRBOA Resolution No. 30, Series of 2022 — Annex A**, which adopted the revised LECPA syllabi and Tables of Specifications (TOS) effective October 2022, and cross-checks the baseline against a 2026 CPALE coverage summary.

The Reviewer App should treat this as a **versioned board-exam blueprint**, not as a permanently frozen body of substantive law or standards. The official syllabus expressly provides that new laws, standards, jurisprudence, regulations, and other issuances effective as of the examination date may supersede related syllabus content unless the Board advises otherwise.

# 2. Financial Accounting and Reporting (FAR)
**Official exam items:** 70

## 2.1 Official syllabus areas

### 1.0 Development of Financial Reporting Framework, Standard-Setting Bodies and Regulation of the Accountancy Profession
- standard-setting bodies: IASB, IFRIC/SIC, FRSC, PIC
- regulation of the accountancy profession in the Philippines
- PRBOA, accredited professional organization, sectors of practice and accreditation

### 2.0 Conceptual Framework, Accounting Process and Presentation of Financial Statements
- Conceptual Framework
- qualitative characteristics
- financial-statement elements, recognition, derecognition and measurement
- capital and capital maintenance
- accounting process/cycle
- journals, ledgers, worksheets, adjusting/closing/reversing entries
- Statement of Financial Position
- Statement of Comprehensive Income
- Statement of Changes in Equity
- Statement of Cash Flows
- Notes
- basic and diluted EPS

### 3.0 Cash and Other Financial Assets
- cash, bank reconciliation, petty cash
- FVTPL, FVOCI, amortized-cost financial assets
- receivables, doubtful accounts, transfer of receivables
- bond investments
- investments in associates and joint ventures

### 4.0 Non-financial Assets
- inventories
- property, plant and equipment
- borrowing costs
- depreciation, revaluation and impairment
- investment property
- intangible assets
- biological assets
- non-current assets held for sale
- prepaid expenses and other assets

### 5.0 Financial Liabilities
- classification and initial recognition
- debt issue costs
- effective-interest method
- troubled debt restructuring

### 6.0 Non-financial Liabilities, Provisions and Contingencies
- loyalty programs
- warranties/guarantees
- unearned revenue
- provisions and contingencies

### 7.0 Shareholders' Equity
- share capital
- treasury shares
- retirement/conversion
- retained earnings
- errors and accounting-policy changes
- dividends
- quasi-reorganization
- accumulated OCI
- book value per share

### 8.0 Other Topics
- share-based payment
- leases
- income taxes
- employee benefits
- interim reporting
- operating segments

### 9.0 Other Reporting Frameworks
- PFRS for SMEs
- PFRS for Small Entities
- reporting for microenterprises

# 8. TOS-wide assessment metadata
The PRBOA TOS uses:
- **PQF Level 6**
- **Difficulty distribution:** Easy 30%, Moderate 40%, Difficult 30%
- **Bloom dimensions:** Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, Creating

These are **assessment metadata**, not the Reviewer App's instructional/scaffolding levels.

# 9. Recommended Reviewer App curriculum model

```
Board Exam / Exam Program
  └── Subject
       └── Syllabus Area
            └── Syllabus Topic
                 └── Syllabus Subtopic
                      ↕ many-to-many
                    Concept
                      ↕
                    Lesson
                      ↕
               Learning Objective
                      ↕
                   Question
```

Separately:

```
Exam Blueprint / TOS
  ├── subject allocation
  ├── area/topic weight
  ├── number of items
  ├── Bloom/cognitive target
  └── difficulty target
```

And:

```
Standards / Laws / Issuances Register
  ├── authority
  ├── effective date
  ├── supersedes / amends
  ├── source
  └── affected syllabus topics
```

The **syllabus defines examinable scope**, the **TOS defines sampling/weighting**, and the **concept graph defines what the learner must understand**.

# 10. Import rules
1. Preserve PRBOA numbering as an external/reference code.
2. Do not automatically treat every numbered syllabus line as a concept.
3. Import subjects → syllabus areas → topics/subtopics first.
4. Import TOS outcomes, weights and item counts separately.
5. Extract atomic concepts in a second curriculum-analysis pass.
6. Allow many-to-many mappings among syllabus topics, concepts, objectives and questions.
7. Version the blueprint and applicability dates.
8. Keep laws, standards and issuances separate and link them to affected syllabus nodes.
9. Never silently overwrite the official baseline when current law changes.
